Highlights

Low cost

About $4.5k for a complete six-drive system, versus over $50k for comparable commercial systems.

Precise and stable

Matched or improved precision and stability compared to commercial systems.

Built for multi-probe recording

Up to three probes per drive and six drives per console.

Open source

Open designs and code for every component.

Abstract

Neuropixels Long probes have ushered in a new era of high-density neurophysiology recording in large brains. Here, we present OpenDrive, an open-source microdrive system designed specifically for acute insertion of Neuropixels Long probes. OpenDrive combines a custom motorized microdrive, six-drive control console, and software platform. Compared with commercially available options, OpenDrive offers (1) low cost, (2) precise, accurate, and stable probe movement, (3) scalability to many-probe recording, and (4) open-source hardware designs and software. In our experiments, OpenDrive has already supported recordings from thousands of neurons across a variety of cortical and subcortical structures. By lowering technical barriers and enabling community-driven development with OpenDrive, we aim to accelerate Neuropixels recording in large brains.
